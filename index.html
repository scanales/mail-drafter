Mail Drafter

Mail Drafter is a small personal tool used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What it does:

  • Reads short notes the owner emails to the tool's own Gmail inbox from one approved address.
  • Turns those notes into draft social media posts.
  • Emails the drafts back to the owner for review. Nothing is posted until the owner approves it by replying.

It uses Google sign-in only to read and send mail in the tool's own Gmail account.
